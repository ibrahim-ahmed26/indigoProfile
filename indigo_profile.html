Who We Are

About Company

Indigo Intelligent Systems – IIS is one of Egypt's most promising ICT companies, specializing in providing a wide spectrum of innovative technology solutions that serve the decision-maker and tailored to their needs in a rapidly changing environment.

IIS has joined the system integration field and provided ICT solutions since 2021. Since then, IIS has been a key partner of world-leading IT–Telecommunication brands, and a pioneering enterprise in IT infrastructure solutions for government organizations, financial sector, banking, insurance, education, and telecommunication.

Currently, IIS is one of the local leading system integration solution and service providers.

What Drives Us

Core Values

How we conduct our business is just as important as what we do. Our core values are the principles that guide us daily in helping our customers achieve financial prosperity and peace of mind.

🤝 Integrity & Ethical Behavior
IIS operates in accordance with the highest ethical standards in all relationships with customers, suppliers, employees, and community. We are honest in everything we do and our reputation is built on trust and integrity.
💡 Innovation & Best Value
IIS fosters a corporate climate open to new technologies, ideas, and opportunities. We encourage innovation in our employees and reward them for thought-leadership and performance that provides best value to customers and community.
👥 Employee Empowerment
IIS empowers employees to determine their own destiny and be involved in the growth and success of the company. We support employee education, act on innovative ideas, and involve them in growing the company.
🌱 Community Support
IIS understands that the success of our local community is vital to the success of our business. We actively promote STEM programs and contribute to local, charitable organizations to build a better tomorrow.
